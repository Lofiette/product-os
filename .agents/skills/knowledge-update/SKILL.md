# knowledge-update

Use after implementation, discovery, verification, or approved decisions change durable product understanding.

## Procedure

1. Identify affected product knowledge artifacts.
2. Do not rewrite the whole map.
3. Preserve evidence, confidence, freshness, unknowns, and review triggers.
4. Update parent artifacts only with links and summaries.
5. Move details to child artifacts or task packets when needed.
6. Compact CHRONICLE after durable updates.

## Output

Knowledge Update Summary:

- artifacts updated;
- evidence added;
- confidence changes;
- stale/needs-review markers;
- task-driven gaps;
- next review trigger.
