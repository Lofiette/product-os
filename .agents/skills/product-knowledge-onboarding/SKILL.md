# product-knowledge-onboarding

Use to onboard an existing, greenfield, or redesigned product into Product Knowledge.

## Modes

- Existing product discovery: discover product shape from repository evidence.
- Greenfield product creation: build hypothesis maps from user-approved decisions and created artifacts.
- Redesign/migration: map current state, target state, delta, risks, and regression boundaries.

## Existing product phases

1. Scope and approval setup.
2. Path-only repository shape scan.
3. App shell / top navigation reads.
4. MVP `PRODUCT_MAP` and `KNOWLEDGE_INDEX`.
5. Top-level area maps.
6. Operational prewarm for high-value/high-complexity areas only.
7. API/data shape contract prewarm when useful.
8. Final compaction and ready state.

## Rules

- `PRODUCT_MAP` is navigation, not an encyclopedia.
- Area maps are routing artifacts, not implementation walkthroughs.
- Flow maps are task-driven unless a high-value flow is repeatedly needed.
- Keep confidence, freshness, evidence, unknowns, and review triggers explicit.
- Target sizes are guidance, not hard truncation caps.
