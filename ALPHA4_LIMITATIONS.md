# Alpha 4 Limitations

- Roles are logical lenses; worker archetypes and real delegation controls are not implemented yet.
- Role routing evals are deterministic metadata/registry proxies, not live Codex traces.
- Quality gates are documented contracts; automated enforcement hooks are a later phase.
- Product Knowledge schemas, claim provenance, and freshness automation are not yet migrated.
- No SQLite registry, MCP servers, external-service adapters, or live observability are included.
- The 50 role methods are substantially deeper than 3.0, but a future domain-method references phase can add curated source libraries and worked examples.
- Parallel write workers, worktrees, cancellation, timeout, and quorum remain out of scope.
