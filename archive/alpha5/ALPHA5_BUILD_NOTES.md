# Alpha 5 Build Notes

Alpha 5 implements the typed Product Knowledge schema and lifecycle over the frozen Alpha 4 expertise baseline.

It adds canonical YAML, generated Markdown projections, claim provenance, source revisions, dependency freshness, task-driven update accounting, Context Packets, and sanitization/sharing metadata. Product Knowledge initializes lazily and remains file-backed; hooks, SQLite, MCP, AST dependency graphs, and external adapters remain later phases.
