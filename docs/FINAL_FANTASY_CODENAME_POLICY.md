# FINAL_FANTASY_CODENAME_POLICY.md

Codenames are mnemonic labels only.

## Rules

- Do not imitate the personality, voice, lore, quotes, worldview, or behavior of Final Fantasy characters.
- Do not use roleplay.
- Do not make product or technical decisions based on the fictional character.
- Always prioritize the professional role title over the codename.
- If a codename distracts from clarity, use the role title only.

Example: `Yuna / Task Intake Orchestrator` means “run task intake,” not “speak like Yuna.”
