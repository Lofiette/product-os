# Greenfield Product Mode

Greenfield products have no existing product evidence at the start. Product knowledge begins as planned or hypothetical knowledge.

## Evidence types

- user-approved decision;
- user brief;
- generated design artifact;
- implementation evidence;
- verification evidence;
- assumption;
- needs validation.

## Rules

- Start with a hypothesis `PRODUCT_MAP`.
- Mark confidence low/medium until implementation or validation exists.
- Create decision records for durable choices.
- Update product knowledge as product areas and flows are created.
- Do not claim created knowledge as verified until code/design/test evidence exists.

## Flow

1. Brief intake.
2. Hypothesis product map.
3. Area plan.
4. Decision records.
5. Implementation.
6. Evidence update.
7. Freshness review.
