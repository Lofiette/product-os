# Product Onboarding

Use product onboarding to create enough product knowledge for future tasks without reading the whole product.

## Modes

1. Existing product onboarding.
2. Greenfield product onboarding.
3. Redesign or migration onboarding.

## Existing product phases

1. Scope and approval setup.
2. Repository shape scan: paths only.
3. Product surface mapping: shell, navigation, top-level route evidence.
4. Area maps: compact routing maps.
5. Operational prewarm: only high-value/high-complexity areas.
6. API/Data Shape contract prewarm: frontend-facing contracts, not backend deep dive.
7. Cleanup: compact CHRONICLE, close onboarding ticket, ready state.

## Completion criteria

Onboarding is sufficient when future tasks can route through `PRODUCT_MAP`, `KNOWLEDGE_INDEX`, and relevant area maps without broad discovery.

It is not necessary to eliminate all unknowns. Unknowns should become task-driven.
