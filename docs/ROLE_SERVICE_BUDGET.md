# ROLE_SERVICE_BUDGET.md — Active Roles, Services, and Consultations

Use this document to avoid fake complexity.

## Categories

### Active specialist role
Counts against the role budget.
Use when the role produces a named artifact that can change the plan, implementation, risk posture, or verification.

### System service
Does not count against the role budget when compact.
Examples:
- Intake service by Yuna for Tiny/Fast Lane.
- Chronicle service update after a small file change.
- Consistency Lite check for Standard planning.
- Review 0/1 lightweight check.

If the service produces a full artifact or coordinates multiple roles, it becomes an active role.

### Consulted role card
Does not count against role budget.
Use when Team Architect reads a role card to decide whether the role should become active.

## Budget examples

Tiny copy change:
- Active: UX Writer.
- Service: compact intake, Review 0, optional chronicle service.
- Do not activate full Chronicle Keeper or Code Reviewer unless risk or ambiguity appears.

Standard MVP:
- Active: Product Strategist, UX Interaction Reviewer, UX Writer, Design System Guardian, Frontend Architect, QA Engineer.
- Service: Chronicle service; Team Architect may coordinate.

High-risk AI agent:
- Active: AI/ML Systems Architect, Model Evaluation Specialist, AI Safety Reviewer, Security Reviewer, Privacy & Compliance Reviewer, relevant architect, QA Engineer, Consistency Auditor.
- Service: Chronicle update may become active Chronicle Keeper if the task spans multiple steps.
