# Professional Interface Review Checklist

## Workflow and data

- Is the unit of work and decision cadence explicit?
- Are object identity, selection scope, permissions, freshness, and lifecycle visible where needed?
- Can users compare the fields that actually drive the decision?
- Does realistic extreme data still preserve meaning and action?

## Expert performance

- Can the most frequent path be completed without unnecessary mode or device switching?
- Are keyboard, bulk, repeat-last, templates, saved views, and macros considered from observed repetition rather than feature hunger?
- Does the layout preserve stable locations and user-arranged state?

## Control and recovery

- Are destructive, cross-page, all-matching, automated, and partial actions unmistakable?
- Can users cancel, undo, inspect history, retry only failed items, and resume interrupted work?
- Are draft, saved, queued, published, partial-success, and conflict states distinct?

## Dashboard and data views

- Does every metric or view support a named decision?
- Are units, definitions, filters, freshness, uncertainty, and exact values available?
- Are linked selections synchronized with a clear scope and reset?

## Acceptance

- Test with realistic volume, long/localized values, permissions, latency, partial failures, narrow/zoomed views, keyboard, and assistive technology.
- Reject visual simplification that removes useful comparison or forces repeated navigation.
