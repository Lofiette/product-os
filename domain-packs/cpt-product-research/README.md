# CPT Product & Research

Product strategy and evidence workflows for product teams.

## Skills

- `cpt-analytics-measurement` — Use to define event taxonomy, metrics, properties, data sources, segmentation, dashboards, and data-quality checks.
- `cpt-customer-journey` — Use to map end-to-end customer journeys, service blueprints, channels, support signals, handoffs, and backstage dependencies.
- `cpt-evidence-research-plan` — Use to plan evidence gathering, source strategy, method, synthesis, and confidence when facts are missing; not to invent findings.
- `cpt-experiment-design` — Use to design A/B tests, pilots, quasi-experiments, hypotheses, guardrails, sample logic, and decision rules.
- `cpt-growth-activation` — Use to design ethical onboarding, activation, retention, conversion, and product growth loops with measurable value.
- `cpt-information-architecture` — Use to design taxonomy, hierarchy, navigation, labeling, grouping, and findability for complex information spaces.
- `cpt-market-research` — Use for market/category, alternatives, competitor, adoption, pricing, and positioning research; not for unsupported market claims.
- `cpt-opportunity-ideation` — Use explicitly for controlled opportunity discovery and ideation with approval-gated scope; not as evidence or mandatory ceremony.
- `cpt-product-scope` — Use to frame product outcome, users, scope, MVP slice, non-goals, acceptance, and success measures; not for code-level planning.
- `cpt-ux-research` — Use to design UX research, usability evaluation, recruiting, protocol, synthesis, and validity; not to treat assumptions as insights.
