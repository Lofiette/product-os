# CPT Evidence Research Plan

## Use when

- A decision depends on market, user, operational, or external facts not yet available.
- External access is unavailable and a research-ready plan is needed.

## Do not use when

- The user asks only for creative ideation.
- Existing evidence is sufficient for the decision.

## Required inputs

- Decision to support, known evidence, unknowns, constraints, audience, timeline, access, and ethics/privacy requirements.

## Method

1. Define the decision and evidence threshold.
2. Decompose unknowns into answerable research questions.
3. Choose primary, secondary, qualitative, quantitative, or mixed methods based on the decision.
4. Define sources/participants, sampling, bias controls, protocol, and data handling.
5. Specify synthesis method, contradiction handling, and confidence rubric.
6. When access is unavailable, produce prioritized sources, search queries, and collection instructions instead of claims.
7. Define stop criteria and how results will change the decision.

## Output contract

Produce a compact artifact containing:

- `Decision/evidence matrix.`
- `Research questions and methods.`
- `Sampling/source/protocol plan.`
- `Synthesis, confidence, caveats, and stop criteria.`

## Evidence standard

- Hypotheses must remain hypotheses until collected evidence supports them.
- Source quality and recency must be recorded.

## Stop and escalate

- Research would collect sensitive data without approved handling.
- The decision cannot be changed by any likely finding.

## Failure modes to avoid

- Choosing interviews for every question.
- Producing a source list without a decision model.
