# CPT Growth Activation

## Use when

- The task concerns first value, adoption, retention, conversion, lifecycle, or referral.

## Do not use when

- The request is generic marketing copy or manipulative engagement.

## Required inputs

- Target segment, product value, journey, funnel/cohort data, constraints, business model, and trust risks.

## Method

1. Define activation as realized user value, not a vanity action.
2. Map acquisition-to-value journey and identify friction, motivation, ability, and trust barriers.
3. Segment by intent, maturity, channel, and lifecycle.
4. Design interventions across product, content, messaging, and operations.
5. Model growth loop inputs, outputs, delays, saturation, and failure modes.
6. Define metrics, guardrails, experiment sequence, and retention cohorts.
7. Reject dark patterns and changes that trade long-term trust for short-term conversion.

## Output contract

Produce a compact artifact containing:

- `Activation/value definition.`
- `Journey/funnel and barrier map.`
- `Growth-loop or lifecycle intervention plan.`
- `Metrics, guardrails, experiments, and ethical risks.`

## Evidence standard

- A conversion lift without user-value evidence is insufficient.

## Stop and escalate

- The requested tactic is deceptive or coercive.
- Core product value is undefined.

## Failure modes to avoid

- Optimizing signup completion while harming activation.
- Confusing notifications with retention.
