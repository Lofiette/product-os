# CPT Market Research

## Use when

- A product decision depends on category structure, alternatives, demand, or competitive differentiation.

## Do not use when

- The task is user usability research inside an existing product.
- No market decision is being made.

## Required inputs

- Market decision, product scope, geography/segment, existing evidence, source constraints, and date horizon.

## Method

1. Define the market/category boundary and target segment.
2. Map direct, indirect, substitute, and status-quo alternatives.
3. Create a source-backed competitor and capability matrix.
4. Analyze adoption drivers, switching costs, procurement, trust, and channel constraints.
5. Treat TAM/SAM/SOM and pricing as assumption models with sources and sensitivity.
6. Derive positioning axes and opportunities without copying competitors.
7. Grade evidence quality, recency, and conflicts.

## Output contract

Produce a compact artifact containing:

- `Category and alternatives map.`
- `Competitor/capability matrix.`
- `Adoption, switching, pricing, and positioning hypotheses.`
- `Evidence grade, unknowns, and implications.`

## Evidence standard

- Every market fact needs a source/date or explicit assumption label.

## Stop and escalate

- The category boundary is unresolved.
- Available evidence is too weak for the requested confidence.

## Failure modes to avoid

- Equating competitors with user needs.
- Presenting top-down market size as a forecast.
