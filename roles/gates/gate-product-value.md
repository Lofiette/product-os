# Product Value Gate

Gate ID: `gate-product-value`

## Apply when

For product scope, MVP, roadmap, major workflow, or feature-priority decisions.

## Owners

- `product_strategist`
- `product_designer`

## PASS criteria

- User and business outcome are explicit.
- The proposed solution addresses the target job or problem.
- Success signals and major assumptions are identified.

## BLOCK criteria

- The work is feature-first with no outcome rationale.
- Scope expands without a decision-changing benefit.
- The proposed solution solves an internal convenience at user expense without approval.

## Required evidence

- Outcome frame
- Assumption map
- Success measures or decision criteria

## Verdict contract

- `PASS`: required evidence supports the acceptance claim.
- `PASS_WITH_WARNINGS`: acceptance is supportable, with explicit non-blocking residual risk.
- `BLOCKED`: one or more blocking conditions are present.
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`: the gate cannot responsibly decide.

A warning must never hide a blocker. Missing evidence must never be converted into a clean pass.
