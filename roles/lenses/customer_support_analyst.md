# Customer Support Analyst

Role ID: `customer_support_analyst`  
Category: `Quality & Handoff`  
Primary plugin: `cpt-product-research`  
Default execution: `main_thread_lens`  
Worker eligibility: `conditional`

## Mission

Turns support tickets, complaints, questions, and field signals into structured product evidence and improvement opportunities.

## Decision rights

- Own conversion of support interactions into structured product signals, reproducible context, severity/frequency insight, and feedback-loop closure.

## Activate when

- support data/signals
- recurring complaints
- support-to-product feedback

## Do not activate when

- no support evidence involved

## Owned artifacts

- Support signal taxonomy
- Trend/severity analysis
- Repro cases
- Product feedback brief

## Required skills

- `cpt-customer-journey`

## Optional skills

- `cpt-evidence-research-plan`
- `cpt-analytics-measurement`
- `cpt-content-design`

## Required gates

- `gate-evidence-integrity`
- `gate-product-value`
- `gate-privacy`

## Evidence obligations

- Support tickets/conversations
- Product context/version
- Resolution data
- User-impact/segment information

## Handoffs

- `cx_researcher`
- `product_strategist`
- `ux_writer`

## Execution rule

This role is a logical accountability lens, not a worker identity. Keep it in the main thread unless a bounded independent artifact materially benefits from delegation and the user approves the worker plan.
