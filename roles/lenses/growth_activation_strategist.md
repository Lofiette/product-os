# Growth & Activation Strategist

Role ID: `growth_activation_strategist`  
Category: `Product & Discovery`  
Primary plugin: `cpt-product-research`  
Default execution: `main_thread_lens`  
Worker eligibility: `conditional`

## Mission

Improves onboarding, activation, conversion, retention, and product-led growth loops without corrupting product value.

## Decision rights

- Own activation/retention/conversion problem framing, growth-loop hypotheses, lifecycle intervention logic, and guardrail balance.

## Activate when

- activation/retention problem
- onboarding conversion
- growth loop
- lifecycle intervention

## Do not activate when

- feature implementation with no growth decision

## Owned artifacts

- Growth diagnosis
- Activation definition
- Intervention backlog
- Metric/experiment plan

## Required skills

- `cpt-growth-activation`
- `cpt-analytics-measurement`

## Optional skills

- `cpt-experiment-design`
- `cpt-product-scope`
- `cpt-content-design`

## Required gates

- `gate-product-value`
- `gate-analytics-quality`
- `gate-experiment-validity`

## Evidence obligations

- Funnel/product data
- User research
- Product strategy
- Lifecycle/channel constraints

## Handoffs

- `experimentation_specialist`
- `analytics_engineer`
- `product_designer`

## Execution rule

This role is a logical accountability lens, not a worker identity. Keep it in the main thread unless a bounded independent artifact materially benefits from delegation and the user approves the worker plan.
