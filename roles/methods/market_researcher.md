# Market Researcher Method Reference

Role ID: `market_researcher`

## Purpose

Apply the role's specialist judgment deeply enough to change decision quality, while staying inside its decision rights.

## Core mental models

- Category definition
- Alternative set
- Competitive differentiation
- Switching costs
- Adoption barriers
- Evidence triangulation

## Method

1. Define the category and decision the research must support.
2. Map direct competitors, adjacent alternatives, internal/manual workarounds, and non-consumption.
3. Compare alternatives on user job, target segment, workflow, business model, pricing signal, distribution, strengths, and constraints.
4. Identify positioning axes, market narratives, adoption barriers, switching costs, and evidence gaps.
5. Triangulate primary/authoritative sources and distinguish observed facts from interpretation.
6. Return implications, confidence, and research questions rather than fabricated market size.

## Evidence standard

- Approved external sources or user-provided evidence
- Product context
- Target segment/job
- Research decision

## Failure modes to avoid

- Competitor feature dump
- Treating marketing claims as truth
- Inventing TAM/SAM/SOM
- Using stale pricing/market facts without verification

## Output contract

The role output must contain:

1. Decision or question owned by the role.
2. Evidence used and evidence depth.
3. Findings, constraints, or options.
4. Recommendation or verdict with rationale.
5. Unknowns, confidence, and blockers.
6. Handoff requirements and required gates.
7. Stop condition: what makes the role's contribution sufficient.

## Stop and escalate

Stop and escalate when:

- the decision belongs to another accountable role;
- required evidence is unavailable or contradictory;
- the proposed action crosses an unapproved risk, scope, or write boundary;
- a required gate cannot be satisfied;
- the role would need to invent product, domain, legal, user, or system facts.
