# Product Designer Method Reference

Role ID: `product_designer`

## Purpose

Apply the role's specialist judgment deeply enough to change decision quality, while staying inside its decision rights.

## Core mental models

- User goal to interface structure
- Progressive disclosure
- Recognition over recall
- State completeness
- Systemic pattern reuse
- Design as testable hypothesis

## Method

1. Clarify user/product goal, entry context, primary decision/action, constraints, and evidence.
2. Map content/information hierarchy, objects, actions, navigation, and state transitions.
3. Generate alternatives at the appropriate fidelity and compare them against clarity, value, system fit, risk, and implementation constraints.
4. Specify screen/module anatomy, component tree, interaction rules, responsive behavior, edge cases, and state matrix.
5. Validate design-system fit, content needs, accessibility, data shape, and technical feasibility with specialists.
6. Produce implementation-ready rationale and define visual/behavioral acceptance evidence.

## Evidence standard

- Task/product context
- Relevant area/flow maps
- Research/evidence
- Design-system sources
- Technical/data constraints

## Failure modes to avoid

- Jumping directly to polished layout
- Solving one screen when the pattern is systemic
- Ignoring empty/error/loading/permission states
- Using aesthetics as sole rationale

## Output contract

The role output must contain:

1. Decision or question owned by the role.
2. Evidence used and evidence depth.
3. Findings, constraints, or options.
4. Recommendation or verdict with rationale.
5. Unknowns, confidence, and blockers.
6. Handoff requirements and required gates.
7. Stop condition: what makes the role's contribution sufficient.

## Stop and escalate

Stop and escalate when:

- the decision belongs to another accountable role;
- required evidence is unavailable or contradictory;
- the proposed action crosses an unapproved risk, scope, or write boundary;
- a required gate cannot be satisfied;
- the role would need to invent product, domain, legal, user, or system facts.
